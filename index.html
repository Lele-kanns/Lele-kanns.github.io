What-if Studio

Absurd questions. Real science. Escalated to the end of the world.

What-if Studio is a private production tool for a YouTube channel. It writes, animates and edits short videos that answer "What if…?" questions with real science, for example "What if your punch doubled every time?"

The tool is used only by its owner, for the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users, and nobody else can sign in to it.

How the tool uses Google services

  • YouTube: uploads and schedules the owner's videos, sets titles and descriptions, and posts comments under the owner's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics: reads how the owner's videos perform, to make better videos.
  • Google Drive: stores production files such as audio, renders and finished videos in the owner's Drive.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.
